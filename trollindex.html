Troll Party ("the game", "we") is a co-op party platformer for Android. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data the game uses

DataWhyWho processes it

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. Ads and your consent

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your choice before ads or analytics use your data, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Other players

When you play online, other players in your room can see your player name, your penguin colour, your actions in the game, and hear your voice if voice chat is on. Please do not share personal information in names or chat. Streamer Mode in Settings hides room codes and other players' names on your screen.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data.

5. How long data is kept

Progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google according to its retention settings (we use the shortest practical setting). Your guest account can be deleted on request.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Change ad and analytics consent: Settings → Privacy options.
  • Turn off voice chat or text chat in Settings at any time.
  • Delete your data: email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] with the subject "Delete my data" and your player name#tag (shown in the game). We will delete your guest account and related data within 30 days.

7. Third-party services

  • Epic Online Services: epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy
  • Google Firebase and AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections provided by these services. No method is perfectly secure, but we use well-known providers and collect as little as we need.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Big changes will also be shown in the game.
